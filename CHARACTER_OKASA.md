## Short Backstory
Born where old shrine-lands meet the modern city, Okasa was raised by shrine-spirits and learned rites to walk between worlds. They wander alleys and rooftops, protecting forgotten thresholds and nudging humans toward small acts of kindness — often via clever pranks — to restore balance where the city has smothered old magic.

---

- **Threshold Walking:** move through small spiritual gates and concealments
- **Charmcraft:** imbue small objects with protective or luck-based effects
- **Minor Illusion & Glamour:** create playful illusions to distract or teach
- **Spirit Empathy:** sense mood/intent of nearby spirits and restless objects
